Herbal care

Comprehensive care and personal support with medicinal plants

Herbal care begins with a thoughtful conversation to get to know you as a whole person: your body, daily routine, health history, the pressures you face and the strengths you already have.

What does that mean?

Medicinal plants offer a way to work with the body, supporting its natural strengths and its own healing processes.

Herbal care involves creating a personal formula: extracts, teas or other preparations chosen according to your needs, sensitivities, current medications and care goals. The herbal approach is tailored to each person, rather than relying on a standard, ready-made formula.

This approach may be suitable for people experiencing daily stress, anxiety, depression, sleep difficulties, fatigue, chronic digestive conditions or chronic skin conditions. Herbal care may also offer support for women experiencing hormonal imbalances, including period pain, irregular cycles, severe PMS, fertility challenges and endometriosis.

Herbal care can sometimes take longer than treatment with conventional medication. Its aim goes beyond quick symptom relief to gradually support the body’s natural processes and its ability to address underlying concerns over time. The pace varies from person to person, depending on the condition, how long it has been present and the individual response. Herbal care may be used on its own or alongside medical treatment, with care, monitoring and careful judgment.

How it works

First appointment

An in-depth appointment of around two hours, including a thorough conversation about your health history, habits, how you feel and what you hope to achieve. Appointment fee: ₪300.

Your personal formula

Oren prepares a tailored herbal blend, with clear, simple instructions for use. The herbal formula is charged separately, with the price based on the herbs and preparations selected for treatment.

Gentle, ongoing follow-up

Follow-up appointments last around one hour. We review your response, adjust herbs and dosages, and progress at a pace that fits your life. Follow-up fee: ₪120. Care also includes free consultation calls as needed.
