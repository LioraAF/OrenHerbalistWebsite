Herbal workshops

An experience that connects people, plants and seasons.

Workshops offer a hands-on introduction to plants through foraging, smelling, touching and making herbal preparations, along with informal conversations about using medicinal herbs in everyday life.

What happens in a workshop?

Step out of your daily routine and get to know plants through hands-on activities.

A workshop can be focused and informative, or more active and hands-on. Sometimes we take a short walk, sometimes we gather around a table with oils, resins and herbal teas, and sometimes we work with ingredients that each participant takes home.

The aim is to bring attention back to the body, the senses and the natural surroundings, without making the workshop feel like a formal lesson. There is room for learning, practical activities and the group itself.

Workshop options

Hands-on workshops, a talk or a session tailored for you.

Each option can be a one-off session, part of a series tailored to your group’s interests, location, season and available time, or a special date for two.

A workshop made for you

What are you curious to learn?

Everyone is drawn to something different in the world of plants. Together we’ll create a workshop around your interests, whether it’s a session just for you, a special date for two or a group experience.

The practical details

An established workshop format, adapted to your group.

Who is it for?

Company outings, learning days, young adult communities, families, groups of friends, private events and couples looking for a special date.

How many people can take part?

Hands-on workshops are suitable for couples and groups of up to 30 people. The talk has no limit on audience size.

Where do workshops take place?

Come to the Kadita and Upper Galilee area, or invite Oren to another location in Israel: outdoors, in your community, at a guesthouse or in the office.

How is the price set?

The price depends on the number of participants, location, session length, materials and type of activity.

How do we choose the content?

Before the workshop, we discuss the group, the time available and what you hope to get from the experience: discovery, group bonding, learning, time outdoors or making herbal products together to take home.

What changes with the seasons?

The focus may shift between resins, roots, oils, herbal teas, mushrooms and preparations, following what is available and appropriate at the time.

One session or a series?

Book a single session or build a series that develops from one meeting to the next, deepening your connection with plants.

Children’s workshops

Gentle nature activities that encourage children’s curiosity.

Oren can lead a single session or a series in your community, at an event, at a summer camp or in a community space, exploring medicinal plants, scents and colors through hands-on activities.

Children get to know plants through play and experience: making incense or a simple cream for home use, tasting herbal teas and creating a personal blend suited to their age and group.

The workshops have an established format, with the pace and activities adapted to the children’s ages, group size, venue and occasion.
