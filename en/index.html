My story

For me, nature is a place for connection, creativity, healing and, above all, peace.

My name is Oren Emanuel Shoshana, and I’m a clinical herbalist. My practice brings together therapeutic conversation, nutrition and medicinal plants. I explore Israel’s plant life, forage and make herbal preparations, bringing an endless curiosity to my work that I hope to share with others. I live and work in Kadita, in the Galilee hills, where daily life keeps me connected to the land and its plants.

I studied clinical herbalism at Broshim Campus. My work also brings together gardening, traditional orchard cultivation in Israel and leading herbal workshops. In addition to my daily practice, I’m helping to set up a medicinal herb farm in Greece in an advisory and practical role. I also share my knowledge and practical experience with researchers in the field. Every encounter with a plant, a place or a person teaches me something new, and I continue to share what I learn along the way.

Would you like to get to know me, hear me talk about plants and join me outdoors? Get to know me through my videos.

Every plant has its season. Every preparation has its rhythm.

Living close to nature teaches me to follow the seasons and notice what each change in the weather brings. Every plant has its own time to grow and flower. Each part of the plant—roots, leaves, stems and flowers—has its own properties and mix of compounds, which change throughout the year. Foraging is an art of understanding these differences and knowing what, when and how to gather, taking care to preserve the plant’s qualities and allow it to keep growing next season.

I bring the same care to making herbal preparations. Making extracts and distilling essential oils takes patience, attention and familiarity with the raw material. Careful attention to timing, temperature and every stage of the process allows the plant’s qualities to come through in a well-made preparation.

Inside the clinic

From understanding the plant to understanding the person.

In my clinical practice, my connection with plants comes together with the personal story of each person I work with. We begin by talking about your body, health history, diet, sleep and daily routine, to understand what needs attention and what you would like to change.

Based on what I learn, I create a personalized herbal formula in the form of extracts, teas, balms or other preparations, along with recommendations for diet and daily habits. The choice takes account of your needs, sensitivities and current medications, bringing traditional knowledge together with current research.

Care continues through follow-up, adjusting the herbs and dosages to your individual response. Treatment progresses gradually, supporting the body’s natural strengths, with the pace guided by the body’s needs and response.
