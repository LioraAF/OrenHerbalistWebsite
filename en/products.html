Herbal preparations

Extracts, oils and balms, available ready-made or prepared especially for you.

Some preparations are ready-made products, produced in small batches for simple, everyday needs. Others are made especially for you after a consultation, taking into account your needs, health history, the season and your body’s response.

How it works

Sometimes it starts with a product. Sometimes with a consultation.

Ready-made products address common, straightforward needs: balms, oils, sprays and blends prepared in advance, drawing on experience with familiar herbs and their everyday uses.

For more complex or underlying concerns, the process of making a preparation begins with a consultation. Together we explore what needs attention: digestion, sleep, pain, stress, menstrual cycles, skin, heart health or recovery. This guides the choice of plants, preparation method and dosage for the individual.

These examples offer a general introduction to the preparations available in the clinic and product range. They are not recommendations for self-treatment or a substitute for individual guidance.
