# General image backup

Images here are excluded from folder-backed galleries. Move a photo back into its gallery folder to use it again.

Only photos explicitly excluded by the user are stored here:

- `herbal-extracts-in-basket.jpeg`: product basket excluded from workshops.
- `workshops/purple-flower-harvest-upright-1828.jpg`: purple flowers on a stone, excluded from workshops.

- `workshops-kids/`: six general herb and workshop-supply photos moved out of the kids gallery at the user’s request. The five children’s activity photos remain in the gallery.

No automatic curation is performed. Photos in all other gallery folders are included as supplied.
